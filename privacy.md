# Privacy policy

llm-config is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and has no server of its own.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's explicit consent in Google's consent screen, it reads the owner's own Gmail messages and
sends messages the owner approves, using the scopes gmail.readonly and gmail.send, and the owner's email address to confirm the
account.

**Where data goes.** Mail content is read on demand and shown to the owner's own assistant tools on the same computer. The
Google credential is stored encrypted on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared, advertised against, or sent to any third party,
and nothing is stored on a server operated by the developer.

**Control.** The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all access.

**Contact.** The developer contact is the address on the Google consent screen.
